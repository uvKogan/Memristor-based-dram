# Presentation Outline - MBMM (22nm ReRAM as Commodity Main Memory)

**Rebuilt 2026-09-20 (task T6.2) on the corrected 2026-09 dataset.** This outline describes
`presentation_deck.html` as it now stands: **60 slides**, 15 embedded charts, 48 main-line slides
plus 12 backup slides in two appendices.

**Context:** about 60 min pure talk time (Q&A separate, after). Audience: lab colleagues who know
memristors but not this project, plus the advisor. Standard defense structure (Motivation,
Background, Methodology, Results, Contributions, Future Work), with the change log held in
Appendix B for the opening or for a direct question.

**Source of truth.** Every number on every slide is traceable to `Project_Book.typ` and through it
to the frozen result set. The deck never says more than the book says. Where the book attaches a
caveat, the slide carries it too.

**Figures.** All 15 chart slides are generated by `visualize_slides.py` (repo root) into
`results/slide_graphs_rev2026-09/`. Regenerate with:

```
MPLBACKEND=Agg python3 visualize_slides.py
```

It reads `results/rev2026-09_primary_csv/`, `results/system_rev2026-09_primary/`, the
`results/system_rev2026-09_<axis>/_csv/` sensitivity runs, `results/endurance_table.csv` and the
trace provenance sidecars in `benchmarks/`. **No plotted value and no result number quoted in a
chart footnote is hardcoded**: both are loaded or derived at render time, including the 94.3%
gating break-even, the PCM completion percentages, the per-chip leakage, the device service term
and the GPT-2 burst figures. The one documented exception is `CITED_CONSTANTS`, a small table of
*published* figures used only as footnote context (the two fabricated-chip timing pairs and the
6F2 recessed-channel density bound), each with its source named in a comment; nothing in it is a
result of this work and nothing in it is ever plotted. Every loader fails loudly, naming the file
and the key, rather than plotting a default.

**Three things to say out loud, every time they come up:**

1. ReRAM device latency is an **NVSim projection**. The two SILICON rows are the counterweight.
2. Every lifetime figure is a **projection** from a measured write distribution, never a measured
   lifetime.
3. The per-gigabyte power gap is an **upper bound**: DDR5 gets its power-down credit, ReRAM gets
   none.

**The projection caveat is printed on the slide, not left to the speaker.** Every slide that claims
modeled ReRAM is faster than, or latency-competitive with, DDR5 carries a highlighted caveat block
naming all three conditions: at NVSim-projected device timings only; published silicon is 11.9 us
and 379 us and does not complete the LBM window; and ReRAM is slower than DDR5 on the
write-dominated AI burst. That is slides **23, 24, 25, 26, 29 and 44**; the change log's
corresponding cell on slide 58 carries the same qualifier inline. If a future edit adds another
such claim it needs the same block, and `tests/test_presentation_deck.py` will fail until it does.

## Deck mechanics

Unchanged from the previous deck: horizontal track, arrow keys or click the screen edges, swipe on
touch, `N` toggles speaker notes (hidden by default), `Home` and `End` jump to the ends, the URL
hash is the slide number. The progress rail and the slide counter read `slides.length`, so they
track the 60 slides automatically.

The file is standalone: 15 charts are embedded as base64 PNG data URIs, and the only external
reference is the Google Fonts stylesheet the deck already used.

Rebuild after regenerating charts: the deck's slide source and the assembly step are described in
the T6.2 report at `.superpowers/sdd/effervescent-growing-otter/task-T6.2-report.md`.
